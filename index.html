About Us

Peet's Coffee originated in 1966 in Berkeley, USA, and is hailed as the godfather of the coffee world. It sticks to small-batch handmade roasting, bringing coffee lovers an authentic specialty coffee experience and spreading the brand concept of slow, handcrafted coffee.
